# TryHackMe – Defending Humans

This folder contains my personal notes and documentation from the **Defending Humans** room on TryHackMe.

The room focuses on **social engineering attacks on humans**, the weakest element in cyber security, and how a SOC analyst can help mitigate and detect these attacks before and after they slip through.

---

## 🛡️ Topics Covered

- The human element in cyber security and why humans are targeted
- Social engineering fundamentals (trustworthiness and emotional triggers)
- Common attack techniques:
  - Phishing emails
  - Malware downloads (fake updates, fake CAPTCHAs, SEO poisoning, malicious QR codes)
  - Deepfakes and impersonation
  - Other threats (USB drops, physical attacks, insider threats, fake job offers)
- Mitigation vs. Detection as the two pillars of defense
- Key mitigation measures:
  - Anti-phishing solution
  - Antivirus / EDR solution
  - "Trust but verify" principle
  - Security awareness training
- Practical SOC analyst decision-making using a simulated Security Dashboard
- Building and prioritizing a corporate security policy
