# TryHackMe – SOC Fundamentals

This folder contains my personal notes and documentation from the **SOC Fundamentals** room on TryHackMe.

The room introduces the fundamentals of a **Security Operations Center (SOC)**, focusing on how a dedicated security team continuously monitors an organisation's network, detects suspicious activity, and responds to it before it causes damage.

---

## 🛡️ Topics Covered

- What a SOC (Security Operations Center) is and why it matters
- Purpose and core focus of a SOC: Detection and Response
  - Detecting vulnerabilities, unauthorized activity, policy violations, and intrusions
  - Supporting the incident response process
- The three pillars of a mature SOC: **People**, **Process**, and **Technology**
  - People: SOC team roles and hierarchy (SOC Analyst Level 1/2/3, Security Engineer, Detection Engineer, SOC Manager, CISO)
  - Process: alert triage using the 5 Ws (What, When, Where, Who, Why), reporting, incident response, and forensics
  - Technology: security solutions such as SIEM, EDR, and Firewalls
- Practical exercise: acting as a Level 1 Analyst to triage a real alert using a SIEM solution
