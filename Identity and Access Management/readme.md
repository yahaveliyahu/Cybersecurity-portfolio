# TryHackMe – Identity and Access Management

This folder contains my personal notes and documentation from the **Identity and Access Management** room on TryHackMe.

The room introduces the fundamentals of **Identity and Access Management (IAM)**, focusing on how systems identify users, verify their identity, control what they can access, and hold them accountable for their actions.

---

## 🔐 Topics Covered

- The IAAA model:
  - Identification
  - Authentication
  - Authorisation
  - Accountability
- Authentication factors (something you know, have, and are)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) and 2FA
- The difference between Authorisation and Access Control
- Logging, log forwarding, and SIEM
- Identity Management (IdM) vs Identity and Access Management (IAM)
- Attacks against authentication:
  - Replay attacks
  - Defences such as timestamps and challenge-response
- Access control models:
  - Discretionary Access Control (DAC)
  - Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
  - Mandatory Access Control (MAC) – AppArmor and SELinux
- Single Sign-On (SSO) and its trade-offs
